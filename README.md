# Notes and Stories

Welcome to Notes and Stories I share with the World, even if no one reads them.\
Freely readable and shareable under [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/).


## About

Hi I'm Edim.👋\
This is a place where I share notes, stories, and come to de-clutter my mind.\
Some pieces are finished, many are works in progress, and that's intentional. I still don't know what will get published, so stay tuned.

## What's in here (or better yet, what will eventually be here...)

- `notes/`: short thoughts, observations, things I'm working through
- `stories/`: personal stories and narrative pieces
- `articles/`: longer, more polished writing
- `assets/`: images used in the writing

Files are named by date, like `2026-10-18-title.md`, and other than that I don't have any sense of where it might go from here.

## Reading and following along

Best advice I can give right now is to follow the dates, follow the titles - they might give you some insight on when the idea started to form, and what it's about.

## A note on people and privacy

Everything you read here is drawn from real life. Where other people appear, I change
or omit names and identifying details. If you spot something that should
be removed, please open an issue or contact me.

## Contributing

This is a personal writing project, so I'm not looking for edits to the
writing itself. Typo fixes and kind feedback via issues are welcome.
