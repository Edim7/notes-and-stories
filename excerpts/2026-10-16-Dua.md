I heard a dua I wanted to share.\
This dua was recited publicly by a person who had to abandon his college education (at the time) to grab a gun and defend his country, his city and his people.

# Dova na Bosanskom

„Molimo te Bože da učiniš svako mjesto gdje žive muslimani sigurnim i svetim.\
Podari džennet svim šehidima koji su od Poslanika s.a.v.s. do danas poginuli.\
Učini ponosnom i svetom našu zemlju Palestinu.\
Učini ponosnim sve muslimane na svijetu, da hodaju uzdignute glave, da se ne stide svog porijekla i da se njihovim djevojčicama ne skidaju marame sa glave – kao što je to slučaj ovih dana.\
Gospodaru kod tebe je moć, sva moć ovog svijeta. Zaštiti svako muslimansko dijete širom svijeta koje je ugruženo.\
Gospodaru ove pametne muslimane, koji su ovako lijepo odjeveni učini ponosom na danu suda kada stanu kod Poslanika, da su učinili svoj hizmet za zajednicu sa svoj umet.\
Gospodaru sastavi nas u džennetu sa našim Poslanikom a.s. i sa svim našim šehidima.“

# Dua in English

“Dear God, we pray that You make every place where Muslims live safe and sacred.\
Grant Paradise (Jannah) to all the martyrs who have died from the time of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) until today.\
Make our land, Palestine, proud and sacred.\
Make all Muslims around the world proud, so they can stand tall with their heads held high, to never feel ashamed of their origins, 
and so that their girls do not get their headscarves forcibly removed — as it has been happening in recent days.\
O Lord, all power belongs to You; all the power in this world is Yours. Protect every Muslim child around the world who is in danger.\
O Lord, make these intelligent and beautifully dressed Muslims a source of pride on the Day of Judgment, when they stand before the Prophet, knowing that they have served their community and their ummah (the Muslim community).
O Lord, reunite us in Paradise with our Prophet (peace be upon him) and with all our martyrs.”
